Derive and visualize the charging response of an RC circuit. First, draw a simple RC series circuit with a voltage source V zero, resistor R, and capacitor C. Second, write Kirchhoff's voltage law V zero equals R times i plus V of t. Third, use i equals C times dV over dt to get V zero equals R C dV over dt plus V of t. Fourth, solve this first-order linear ODE to get V of t equals V zero times one minus e to the minus t over R C. Fifth, plot V of t on axes showing exponential rise from zero approaching V zero asymptotically. Sixth, mark the time constant tau equals R C where V reaches 63 percent of V zero. Seventh, mark five tau where V is approximately 99 percent charged.
