Second, write the Fourier series formula f of x equals four over pi times the sum of sine of two k minus one x over two k minus one for k from one to infinity. Third, plot the first harmonic four over pi sine x. Fourth, add the third harmonic four over pi sine three x over three. Fifth, add the fifth harmonic. Sixth, add the seventh harmonic. Seventh, add the ninth harmonic. Each new partial sum should be a different color and approach the square wave more closely. Mention the Gibbs phenomenon at the discontinuities.
