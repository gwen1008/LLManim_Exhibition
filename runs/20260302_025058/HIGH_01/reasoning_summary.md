============================================================
**Concept Extraction**: Linear function y = 2x - 1. Slope m = 2. y-intercept b = -1. The slope means rise/run = 2/1. Need to visualize this ratio dynamically.

**Selected Visual Metaphor (Strategy B)**: A "slope triangle" that is created at one point on the line and then slides to another, proving the slope is constant.

**Spatial Plan & Zone Assignment**:
- **HEADER Zone (y ~ 3.2)**: Title with the function.
- **CONTENT Zone (y from -2.5 to 2.5)**: The main stage. Contains:
    1. Coordinate axes (centered, scaled appropriately).
    2. The line graph.
    3. The primary slope triangle (large, for clarity).
    4. Point labels and slope annotation.
- **FOOTER Zone (y ~ -3.0)**: Explanatory text for the current step.

**Coordinate Calculations**:
- Axes: x_range [-3, 3], y_range [-4, 4]. This comfortably fits y = 2x - 1 (e.g., at x=3, y=5; at x=-2, y=-5).
- Line: Plot from x=-2 to x=3.
- Primary Slope Triangle: Start at a clean point on the line, e.g., P0 at (0, -1) (the y-intercept).
    - Run: From P0 to P1 = (1, -1). This is a horizontal segment of length 1.
    - Rise: From P1 to P2 = (1, 1). This is a vertical segment of length 2. (Because 2*1 - 1 = 1).
    - Hypotenuse: From P0 to P2, which lies on the line.
- Triangle vertices: P0(0, -1), P1(1, -1), P2(1, 1).

**Signal Strategy**:
1. Introduce the line.
2. At P0, indicate the point. Animate the "run" of 1 unit to the right.
3. From the end of the run, animate the "rise" of 2 units up, landing on the line.
4. Highlight the formed right triangle and label its sides "run=1", "rise=2".
5. Animate this triangle sliding along the line to a new position (e.g., starting at (1,1)), demonstrating the ratio is preserved.

**Overflow Prevention**:
- The main visual (axes, line, triangle) stays centered in the CONTENT zone.
- The equation title is in the HEADER.
- Step-by-step narration is placed in the FOOTER and replaced/updated, not stacked.
- The slope annotation ("m = rise/run = 2") will be placed near the triangle, scaled appropriately.
============================================================
