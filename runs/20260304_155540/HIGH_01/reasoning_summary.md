============================================================
**Concept Extraction**: Linear function y = 2x - 1. Slope m = 2, y-intercept b = -1. The pedagogical goal is to visualize slope as rise/run = 2/1.

**Visual Metaphor (Selected)**: Abstract coordinate plane with stepped decomposition.

**Spatial Plan**:
- **HEADER Zone (y ~ 3.0)**: Title and function display.
- **CONTENT Zone (y ~ 0)**: Main coordinate plane and graphical constructions.
- **FOOTER Zone (y ~ -3.0)**: Explanatory text for the current step.

**Key Objects & Coordinates**:
1.  **Axes**: Centered at ORIGIN. x_range [-3, 3], y_range [-4, 4]. Scale ~1.8.
2.  **Line**: Graph of y = 2x - 1. Will be plotted.
3.  **Anchor Point**: Choose the y-intercept (0, -1) as the starting point for steps.
4.  **Step Construction**: From (0, -1):
    - **Run Step**: Move 1 unit right to (1, -1). Draw horizontal arrow.
    - **Rise Step**: Move 2 units up from (1, -1) to (1, 1). Draw vertical arrow.
    - This lands on the line at point (1, 1).
5.  **Triangle**: Form right triangle with vertices (0,-1), (1,-1), (1,1) to encapsulate rise/run.
6.  **Slope Label**: Place near the triangle: "slope = rise/run = 2/1 = 2".

**Constraints**:
- All graphical elements (line, points, steps) stay within the CONTENT zone.
- Explanations are concise and placed in the FOOTER.
- Use color coding: Line (WHITE), Run step (GREEN), Rise step (RED), Triangle fill (BLUE with low opacity).

**Signal Strategy**:
- Step 1: Introduce axes and function.
- Step 2: Plot the line.
- Step 3: Isolate a point on the line (y-intercept).
- Step 4: Animate the "run" step (horizontal move).
- Step 5: Animate the "rise" step (vertical move), completing the step.
- Step 6: Highlight the resulting right triangle and label the slope.
============================================================
