**Concept Extraction**: Linear function y = 2x - 1. Slope m = 2. Rise/Run = 2/1. Graph on coordinate plane. Visualize slope via a right triangle formed by two points on the line.

**Visual Metaphor**: The slope triangle. A right triangle whose legs represent the run (horizontal change) and rise (vertical change). The ratio of these lengths is the slope.

**Spatial Plan (High-Low Split)**:
- **HEADER Zone (y ~ 3.0)**: Title.
- **CONTENT Zone (y ~ 0)**: Axes and main graph. This is the primary visual stage.
- **FOOTER Zone (y ~ -2.8)**: Explanatory text for the current step.

**Key Coordinates & Objects**:
1.  **Axes**: Centered at ORIGIN. x_range [-5,5], y_range [-5,5]. Scale to fit CONTENT zone.
2.  **Line**: Graph of y=2x-1. Will be drawn.
3.  **Points**: Choose two points on the line with an integer run of 1 for clarity.
    - Let x1 = 1, then y1 = 2*1 - 1 = 1. Point A(1,1).
    - Let x2 = 2, then y2 = 2*2 - 1 = 3. Point B(2,3).
    - Run = x2 - x1 = 1. Rise = y2 - y1 = 2.
4.  **Slope Triangle**: Right triangle with vertices at A(1,1), B(2,3), and C(2,1). Leg AC (horizontal) is run. Leg CB (vertical) is rise.
5.  **Labels**: "rise = 2", "run = 1" placed next to the respective legs. Slope formula m = rise/run = 2/1 = 2 displayed prominently.

- Step 3: Mark points A and B. Emphasize they are on the line.
- Step 4: Construct the slope triangle (dashed lines for legs). Indicate the right angle.
- Step 5: Label the rise and run. Then morph these labels into the slope formula m = 2/1 = 2.

**Overflow Prevention**:
