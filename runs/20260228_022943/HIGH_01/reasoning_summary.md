    *   **Step 2**: Choose two clear points on the line to define a "slope triangle". For visual clarity, use x=0 (y=-1) and x=1 (y=1). This gives a run of 1 and a rise of 2.
    *   **Step 3**: Construct the triangle:
        *   Point A: (0, -1, 0) - The y-intercept.
        *   Point B: (1, -1, 0) - Directly right of A (run leg).
        *   Point C: (1, 1, 0) - Directly above B (rise leg).

        *   Draw the hypotenuse (A->C) as a solid segment of the main line.
    *   **Step 4**: Annotate the triangle:
        *   Add Braces for the run (length 1) and rise (length 2).
        *   Label the braces "Run = 1" and "Rise = 2".
